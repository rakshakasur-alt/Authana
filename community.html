Why is it "Messy"?

arrow_drop_down

We believe real food isn't perfectly styled. It's cooked with heart, splashed on the stove, and tastes like index. The "mess" is the process of genuine cooking, not a factory assembly line.

Where do you deliver?

arrow_drop_down

Currently, we are keeping our radius tight to ensure the food stays hot and fresh. We serve the downtown core and university district. Check the menu page for exact boundaries.

Can I request modifications?

arrow_drop_down

Our menu is fixed daily based on what's fresh at the market. However, we always offer a vegetarian alternative. We cannot accommodate complex allergies due to our shared kitchen space.
